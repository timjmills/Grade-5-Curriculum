Grammar (Sun) — Action Verbs

OBJ: Identify action verbs and use them in sentences

Packets — Low (K-1): K: L2.3 Verbs, G1: L1.2 Verbs · Mid (2-3): G2: L1.4 Verbs, G3: L1.4 Verbs · High (4-6): G4: L1.3 Verbs, G5: L1.10 Action Verbs, G6: L1.11 Action Verbs

Body verbs: breathe, pump, digest, blink, flex.

Writing — Revise for flow; peer feedback (2 stars and a wish)

Students reread the draft aloud ("say it before you fix it"), self-check against the co-built rubric, then partner "2 stars and a wish"; revise for flow by adding/smoothing transition words. Grammar link: adverbs (how/when/where).

TWR: sentence combining as the revision move ("I went to the pool. I was scared." becomes "I was scared when I went to the pool.") — each student combines ONE pair and varies one sentence opener.

Mentor: A Different Pond by Bao Phi (Padlet Wk 2) — read one page, hunt for one place to add a tiny detail.

Resources: Writing Process Checklist + Paragraph Rules (Padlet Wk 3) — transition-word bank

IXL: Choose the right end mark (G1) — Combine sentences (G3) — Stretch: Compound sentences (G4) — Varied sentences (G5)

Grammar (Tue) — Adjectives

OBJ: Use adjectives to describe nouns with precision

Packets — Low (K-1): K: L5.2 Using Telling Words to Describe, G1: L1.4 Adjectives · Mid (2-3): G2: L1.5 Adjectives, G3: L1.6 Adjectives and Articles · High (4-6): G4: L1.6 Adjectives, G5: L1.14 Adjectives, G6: L1.16 Adjectives

G3 includes articles (a/an/the).

Writing — Edit, publish, celebrate (EXIT TASK)

Model a final edit against the co-built rubric: past tense, capitals, full stops, best-try spelling. Students edit, then publish a neat written/typed copy with optional illustration.

Author's chair: each writer reads their small moment aloud; classmates give warm "2 stars" feedback; celebrate the published pieces. Early finishers begin a second small-moment story.

TWR: listener summary sentence — "___'s story was about the time ___." (Low/Med say it; High write it).

Resources: Personal Narratives unit doc (Padlet Wk 1) — co-built rubric
