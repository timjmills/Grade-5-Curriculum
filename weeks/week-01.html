How We Learn Best · The Brain

IXL this week:

Low: The Five Senses (Gr 1) · High: Body Systems — Perception & Motion (Gr 4)

Stretch: Body Systems: Digestion (Gr 5) · Circulation & Respiration (Gr 5)

Entry Point (Day 1 — Sunday):

Entry Point — Physical Challenge + “Inside Us” Challenge · done on the SUNDAY the unit starts (Day 1)

Epic bookshelf (Gr 2-4, new): Looking at Layers: Looking Inside the Human Body · Your Digestive System

Part 1 — Physical Challenge: the ‘60-Second Physical Activity Challenges’ (Youth Sport Trust) — a fun ‘compete against yourself’ hook. Ask: “What is happening inside your body as you move — your heart, lungs, muscles?”

Part 2 — ‘Inside Us’ Challenge: students predict and place what they think is inside the body. Launch the mission — build a life-size model of your own body and present & explain it at the Body Systems Museum in Week 3 (the ‘Human Body with Explanation’ exit task).

IXL this week (new): Low: Human Organs & Their Functions (Gr 3, review) · High: Circulation & Respiration (Gr 4, review)

IXL stretch (Gr 4-5, new): Body Systems: Perception & Motion (Gr 5) · Human Organs & Their Functions (Gr 5)

Resources: 60-Second Physical Activity Challenges (YST) · ‘Inside Us’ body organs (PDF)

Mon · Hook + Investigate

▶ Hook video: National Geographic ‘Brain 101’ (≈4 min)

Vocab: brain, neuron, memory, mindset.

Neuromyths — True or False? (10 mins): sort statements — “We only use 10% of our brain”, “Some people are just left-brained.” Reveal they are myths (kept). Brainwave – The Brain PPT — Padlet.

Investigate — Making Connections (20 mins): circle game with wool — pass to a connected word and hold on; the web models how neurons connect and how we learn (kept).

Tue · Apply + Record

Apply — Label the brain: parts & jobs (‘Label Brain / Facts’ SeeSaw — Padlet).

Growth mindset: watch ‘The Power of Yet’ (Padlet); sort fixed vs growth statements (kept).

Epic read: ‘Magic School Bus: Inside the Human Body’ (Epic).

Wed · Review + Check

Review: brain parts + growth mindset; quick check.

Memory lab (10 mins): try memory strategies (chunking, rehearsal) — which helped most? (kept)

Launch the Body Project: each explorer chooses the body to build and which systems to feature in the Week-3 presentation.

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed.

Closing Circle (SELG)

Whole Class Reflection: What is one positive/good thing that happened today? What is something you think you could have tried harder at today? What are you most looking forward to tomorrow at school?

Whole Class Reflection: What is one thing you can't wait to share with someone at home about your school day? What was your happiest/most exciting part of the day? What do you think could have been better today and why?

Early Dismissal

Yoga Ball Pass — each team lies on their backs with legs on the wall; starting at one end, pass a yoga ball using only feet to the next person. If the ball drops, start over. Goal: reach the end of the line fastest.
