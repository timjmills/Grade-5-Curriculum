3-day plan (Mon–Wed)

Stars & the Life Cycle of a Star

IXL this week:

Low: The Night Sky (Gr 1, review) · High: Brightness of the Sun and Other Stars (Gr 4)

Stretch: Brightness of the Sun and Other Stars (Gr 5) · Constellations & the Changing Night Sky (Gr 5)

Review / catch-up — revisit last week's Explorer learning; finish unfinished investigations or SeeSaw posts; preview this week's topic.

Mon · Hook + Investigate

▶ Hook video: National Geographic ‘Stars 101’ (≈4 min)

Vocab: star, nebula, supernova, constellation.

What Do We Know About Stars? Starter (5 mins): “What comes to mind when you hear the word star?” Watch ‘Stars in the Solar System | Science for Kids’ (Padlet); record 3–5 ideas on the board.

Group Mind Map (10 mins): mixed-ability groups make a “Stars” mind map with 3–4 branches (what they are, what they do, questions, the Sun). Support with visuals / sentence starters.

Objective: Describe what stars are and share initial ideas and questions about them.

Assessment: Contribute at least one idea or question to the group ‘Stars’ mind map.

Differentiation: contribute one idea using a sentence starter + adult support / contribute an idea or question independently using a sentence starter / contribute and explain an idea about stars independently

Tue · Apply + Record

The Life Cycle of a Star — Hook (5 mins): show a 1-minute animation. “Did you know stars could die? What did you notice?” ‘Life Cycle of a Star | KS2’ (STEM and Beyond) / NASA eClips: Launchpad — Life Cycle of a Star — Padlet.

Apply (25 mins): overview the 4 simplified stages with flashcards, then label them on the Star Life-Cycle worksheet (differentiated, Padlet). Design a constellation.

Prior-year slides: Life Cycle of a Star PPT (Padlet · Week 4)

Worksheet link: Star Life-Cycle Worksheet (Padlet · Week 4 · Google Drive)

Objective: Sequence and label the 4 stages of a star's life cycle.

Assessment: Label the 4 stages of the star life-cycle worksheet in the correct order.

Differentiation: label stages using a matching word bank + adult support / label stages independently with a word bank / sequence and explain all 4 stages independently without a word bank

Wed · Review + Check

Review: stars vs planets; order the star life cycle; quick check.

Quick check: “Why does the Sun look brighter than the other stars?” — the Sun is a star too, but it looks much brighter only because it is so much closer to Earth than any other star.

Showcase: star/constellation lines; rehearse in small groups.

Objective: Explain the difference between stars and planets and why the Sun looks brighter than other stars.

Assessment: Quick check: “Why does the Sun look brighter than the other stars?”

Differentiation: answer with a sentence frame + adult support / answer independently with a sentence frame / explain distance as the reason, in own words, independently

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed.
