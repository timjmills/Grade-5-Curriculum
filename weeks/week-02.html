Writing — Build the middle: events in order, past tense, detail

Oral rehearsal first: retell the small moment with transition words. Model feeling and five-senses details from a personal story, then students draft the middle on the organizer. Grammar link: past-tense verbs + adjectives.

Mentor revisit: Roller Coaster by Marla Frazee (Padlet Wk 2) — how does she stretch ONE moment into ordered events? TWR: dot-jot 3 events, say each sentence from the jot, then write; expand one flat sentence ("It was fun.") with when/where/why.

Differentiation: High starts one sentence per section, then expands each to three.

Resources: Hamburger Paragraph template (Padlet Wk 3) — BrainPOP Jr: Writing a Paragraph — feeling + five-senses word banks

IXL: Use time-order words (G2) — Regular past tense (G3) — Descriptive details (G3) — Stretch: Irregular past (G4) — Irregular past (G5)

Writing — Write the ending; complete the rough draft

Model an ending that answers two questions: How did it end? How did I feel? Students write endings, then reread the organizer/draft for beginning-middle-end.

TWR: because/but/so for the ending — "I felt ___ because... / I wanted to stop, but... / I was brave, so..." — the best one feeds the ending sentence (Unit 1 stem bank in the TWR Resource Pack).

Stretch: ready students add one line of dialogue after the Powtoons Dialogue video (Padlet Wk 5) — a "4 — above and beyond" choice, not a requirement.

Resources: Personal Narratives unit doc
