EXPLORER

3-day plan (Mon–Wed)

Energy Around Us

IXL this week:

Low: Light and Sound to Communicate (Gr 1) · High: Introduction to Energy (Gr 4) · Extra: Predict Heat Flow (Gr 2)

Stretch: Energy Transformation (Gr 5) · Conductors and Insulators (Gr 5)

Entry Point (Day 1 — Sunday):

Entry Point — Earthquake Drill + Natural Disasters · done on the SUNDAY the unit starts (Day 1)

Entry hook (Sunday, Day 1): run an earthquake ShakeOut drill and watch real disaster documentary footage (short segment); talk about natural disasters. Ask: “What forces are at work — and could we design something that survives?”

Set the mission: the unit ends by planning and building a house that can withstand natural disasters — with at least 4 design-and-build sessions (each: 10–20 min review/teach, then 20–25 min to design & build).

Mon · Hook + Investigate

▶ Hook video: ‘The Power of Sunlight’ — SciShow Kids (real host + demo, ~4 min)

Vocab (kept): energy, light, heat, sound, electrical.

Investigate (kept): ‘What is energy?’ Hunt for energy in the room; energy & food chains — energy comes from the Sun.

Objective: Identify examples of light, heat, sound, and electrical energy in the classroom and explain that the Sun is the original source of most energy on Earth.

Assessment: Point to or name a classroom item and state which type of energy it uses or gives off, then trace it back to the Sun.

Differentiation: Point to and name one energy example with adult support / name two energy types and describe them with some support / independently name, sort, and trace multiple energy examples back to the Sun.

Tue · Apply + Record

Apply (kept): sort the types of energy; ‘where does food energy begin?’ Record on SeeSaw.

SeeSaw sort: Energy types SeeSaw activity — ADD PADLET LINK

Objective: Sort objects and examples by energy type and explain how food energy begins with the Sun.

Assessment: Complete the SeeSaw sort correctly and answer: Where does food energy begin?

Differentiation: Sort with a labelled word bank and adult support / sort independently with some prompting / sort independently and explain the Sun-to-food energy chain in a full sentence.

Epic read: ‘Forms of Energy’ (Epic collection).

Wed · Review + Check

Review the forms of energy; quick check.

Resources: BrainPOP: Forms of Energy · Reading (Gr 2-3): 'Energy All Around Us' (Readings Pack) · Readings Pack — ADD PADLET LINK

Objective: Recall and name the forms of energy studied this week (light, heat, sound, electrical).

Assessment: Answer a quick oral or written check: name one form of energy and give one example of it.

Differentiation: Choose the correct energy word from a picture/word bank with support / name one form of energy independently / name and give an example of two or more forms of energy independently.

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed.
