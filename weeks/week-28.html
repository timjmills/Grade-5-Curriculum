Writing — Write the beginning

A strong beginning introduces the character in the setting, then the problem. Model drafting the class story's beginning from the story-map; students write theirs. Grammar link: past-tense verbs — narratives are told in the past.

TWR: write from the jots — each beginning jot becomes a sentence; expand the opening sentence with where and when; the Week 1 appositive character-introduction leads it off.

Resources: How to Catch a Star — read aloud (Padlet mentor read-alouds)

IXL: Descriptive details (G2) — Character emotions and traits (G3) — Stretch: Narrative point of view (G4) — Varied sentences (G5)

Grammar (Tue) — Synonyms & Antonyms

OBJ: Identify synonyms and antonyms; use synonyms to improve word choice

Packets — Low (K-1): K: L4.7 Antonyms, G1: L3.11 Synonyms, G1: L3.12 Antonyms · Mid (2-3): G2: L3.18 Synonyms, G2: L3.19 Antonyms, G3: L3.18 Synonyms and Antonyms · High (4-6): G4: L3.16 Synonyms and Antonyms, G5: Review: Ch. 3 Lessons 1-9 (word choice review), G6: L3.4 Synonyms and Antonyms

Big/enormous/vast are synonyms.

Writing — Write the middle: "Show, Don't Tell"

Model turning a flat sentence ("She was scared.") into a Show-Don't-Tell sentence ("Her hands shook as she stared at the door.") with feeling and sense words; students write the middle — events in order — with at least one Show-Don't-Tell sentence.

TWR: show-don't-tell = expansion with How? — interrogate the flat sentence: How do we know? What did her hands do?

Differentiation: Low/Med feeling-word bank + frame ("___ felt ___ because ___."); High adds two Show-Don't-Tell sentences.

Resources: Show, Don't Tell — "Show, Don't Tell" 16-chart pack (Padlet charts) — word bank: feeling + five-senses words

The Moon — Phases & Craters

IXL this week:

Low: The Night Sky (Gr 1) · High: Identify Phases of the Moon (Gr 4)

Stretch: Identify Phases of the Moon (Gr 5) · Lunar Eclipses (Gr 5)

Review / catch-up — revisit last week's Explorer learning; finish unfinished investigations or SeeSaw posts; preview this week's topic.

Mon · Hook + Investigate

▶ Hook video: National Geographic ‘Moon 101’ (≈4 min)

Vocab: phase, crater, surface, gravity, lunar month.

Why Does the Moon Change Shape? Starter (7 mins): show Moon-phase images — “Sometimes it’s a bright circle, sometimes just a little sliver.” Big Question: “Why does the Moon seem to change its shape?”

Teach: there are 8 phases; the Moon cycles through them in about 29.5 days (a lunar month). Watch ‘Moon 101’ (above) / ‘Tour of the Moon’ (NASA Goddard) + Apollo 11 real landing footage (AP) — Padlet. Phases of the Moon Information.

Objective: Explain that the Moon appears to change shape because it cycles through 8 phases in about 29.5 days.

Explorer photos / resources (from last year's plans):

There are eight phases of the moon

The phases are named after how much of the moon we can see, and whether the amount visible is increasing, or decreasing each day.

Phases of the moon as seen in the Northern Hemisphere

Phases of the moon as seen in the Southern Hemisphere

Tue · Apply + Record

Investigate — Moon Craters: Making an Impact! Materials per group: a tray of flour 2–3 cm deep, cocoa/dark powder sifted on top, 3 plasticine balls. “Space explorers! What do you see on the Moon’s surface — what shapes or marks?” Drop the balls to make impact craters; observe & record size/shape. NASA — craters on the Moon.

Objective: Explain that craters on the Moon's surface form from the impact of objects hitting it.

Apply — Oreo Moon Phases: model the 8 phases with sandwich cookies, then label them (SeeSaw). SciShow Kids: Why Does the Moon Change? (~4 min, replaces dead link) — Padlet.

Book: Moon Phases Collection (Epic). Worksheet: Phases of the Moon Labelling Activity Sheet (Twinkl).

Objective: Model and label the 8 phases of the Moon in the correct order.

Wed · Review + Check

Review: sequence the 8 moon phases (new → full → new); quick check.

Reading (Gr 2-3): 'The Moon Changes Shape' (Grade 2-3 Readings Pack) · Readings Pack — ADD PADLET LINK

Moon Craters book: The Moon (Epic) — Worksheet: Surface of the Moon Labelling Activity Sheet (Twinkl).

Working link (new): The Moon (Epic · from Padlet Day 3 post)

Showcase: moon facts & lines; begin building exhibition models.

Objective: Sequence the 8 Moon phases in order from new moon to full moon and back again.

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed.
