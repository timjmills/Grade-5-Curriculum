Earth, Our Home — Systems & Caring for the Planet

IXL this week:

Low: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (Gr 2) · High: Label Parts of Water Cycle Diagrams (Gr 4) · Extra: Water on Earth (Gr 2)

Stretch: Geosphere, Biosphere, Hydrosphere & Atmosphere (Gr 5) · Describe and Graph Water on Earth (Gr 5)

Review / catch-up — revisit last week's Explorer learning; finish unfinished investigations or SeeSaw posts; preview this week's topic.

Mon · Hook + Investigate

▶ Hook video: National Geographic ‘Earth 101’ (≈3.5 min)

Vocab: Rain, Soil, Plants, Water, Grow, Earth systems, interact; recycle, reduce, reuse.

What Helps Plants Grow? (5 mins): show a plant, a cup of soil and a spray bottle. Ask: “What does a plant need to grow? Where does water come from? What does soil do?” Record responses.

Where Is Earth’s Water? (10 mins): ‘Where Can We Find Water?’ (SciShow Kids) — make a simple bar graph of oceans / ice / lakes & rivers; most of Earth’s water is salt water in the oceans, and only a small share is fresh water (ice, lakes, rivers, underground).

Explicit teaching (10 mins): rain gives water; soil holds water & nutrients; plants absorb them. Teacher model: “Rain gives water to the soil. The soil helps the plants grow.” Build an anchor chart.

Investigate: water cycle in a bag (tape to a sunny window) — watch evaporation & ‘rain’.

Objective: Explain how rain, soil, and plants interact as parts of Earth's water cycle.

Assessment: Complete the anchor chart sentence “Rain gives water to the soil. The soil helps the plants grow” and observe the water-cycle-in-a-bag model.

Differentiation: complete the sentence with a word bank + adult support / complete the sentence independently with a word bank / explain the full rain-soil-plant water cycle in own words independently

Tue · Apply + Record

Apply — Caring for our planet: recycling sort (Community Sorting); then read and make a simple bar graph of class data (most / least).

Resources: Twinkl: Recycling Sorting Activity · NASA Space Place: All About Earth

Record: three actions we can take to look after Earth (SeeSaw sentence: “Communities can ___.”).

Objective: Identify actions people can take to look after Earth, including recycling.

Assessment: Complete the SeeSaw sentence “Communities can ___” with one action to care for Earth.

Differentiation: complete the sentence with a picture/word bank + adult support / complete the sentence independently with a word bank / name three actions and explain how each helps Earth independently

Wed · Review + Check

Review: Earth systems + sustainability; quick check.

Showcase: ‘Caring for Earth’ message lines; full draft of all scripts due.

Objective: Explain how Earth's systems connect and why caring for them (through actions like recycling) matters.

Assessment: Review: Earth systems + sustainability; quick check.

Differentiation: name one Earth system with support / name one Earth system and one caring action independently / explain how Earth's systems connect and why sustainability matters independently

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed.
