Review and Assess

Objective

Students will be able to identify and describe real-world objects that are three-dimensional (3D), and explain that 3D shapes have faces, edges, and vertices.

Students will be able to identify and describe the characteristics of prisms and pyramids, including their base shapes and the shape of their faces, and differentiate between these two types of 3D shapes.

Students will be able to describe the attributes of cylinders, cones, and spheres, and will be able to differentiate between these shapes and those of prisms and pyramids.

Students will be able to combine various 3D shapes to create new structures and will accurately use math vocabulary to describe the attributes of the shapes and the structures they build.
