Writing — Revise for flow; peer feedback (2 stars and a wish)

Students reread the draft aloud ("say it before you fix it"), self-check against the co-built rubric, then partner "2 stars and a wish"; revise for flow by adding/smoothing transition words. Grammar link: adverbs (how/when/where).

TWR: sentence combining as the revision move ("I went to the pool. I was scared." becomes "I was scared when I went to the pool.") — each student combines ONE pair and varies one sentence opener.

Mentor: A Different Pond by Bao Phi (Padlet Wk 2) — read one page, hunt for one place to add a tiny detail.

Resources: Writing Process Checklist + Paragraph Rules (Padlet Wk 3) — transition-word bank
