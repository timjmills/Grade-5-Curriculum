Writing — Search skills: find two reliable sources

Writing — Take notes in your own key words (source 1)

Model reading a short passage and jotting 3-4 key words instead of copying sentences. Students read source 1 and take key-word notes on the organizer, checking no sentence is copied word-for-word.

TWR: key-word notes ARE the TWR move — add the reverse check: say two jots back as full sentences to a partner; if you can't, the note was copied, not understood.
