Forces & Motion

IXL this week:

Low: Identify Pushes and Pulls (Gr 2) · High: Balanced and Unbalanced Forces (Gr 4)

Stretch: Mass, Force and Acceleration (Gr 5) · Design a Balloon-Rocket Experiment (Gr 5)

Review / catch-up — revisit last week's Explorer learning; finish unfinished investigations or SeeSaw posts; preview this week's topic.

Mon · Hook + Investigate

▶ Hook video: ‘Newton’s Laws of Motion’ — STEM and Beyond (real footage, ~3.5 min)

Vocab (kept): force, push, pull, gravity, friction.

Investigate — Gravity (kept): drop objects of different masses — what happens? Why?

Objective: Observe that gravity pulls objects toward the ground and describe what happens when objects of different masses are dropped.

Tue · Apply + Record

Friction investigation (kept): roll/slide a car over different surfaces (carpet, tile, sandpaper) — which slows it most? Measure & record.

Worksheet: Twinkl: Pushing and Pulling Forces (KS2)

Objective: Investigate how different surfaces affect friction and identify which surface slows a moving object most.

Wed · Review + Check

Review forces & motion; quick check.

Extra check: IXL: How do mass and force affect motion? (Grade 3)

Objective: Recall key force vocabulary (push, pull, gravity, friction) and describe how a force changes an object's motion.

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed.
