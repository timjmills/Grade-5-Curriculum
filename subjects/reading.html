1. Curriculum Overview

Reading for 2026-2027 is kept as currently written in the 35 weekly plans; this map replicates those plans so the whole year can be seen in one place. The reading block runs Sunday–Thursday with a daily Guided Reading mini-lesson followed by rotations (small-group practice, RazKids, Epic, IXL/Prodigy, book box and SeeSaw activities).

Spelling is woven into the reading block: word lists are introduced Monday and tested Thursday, using Evan-Moor Building Spelling Skills with a Low (Grade 2) and High (Grade 4) list each week. Unit literacy assessments, DIBELS and phonics screeners anchor progress checks; unit reading Padlets hold the resources.

Below, each unit gives a weekly overview, then the full reading rows (lesson content, objectives, assessment and differentiation) copied verbatim — with live links — from each week's plan.
