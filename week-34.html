EXPLORER

3-day plan (Mon–Wed)

Earth Systems & Disasters → begin the BUILD — Build sessions: ~10–20 min review/teach, then ~20–25 min to design & build.

IXL this week:

Low: Changes to Earth’s Surface — Earthquakes (Gr 2) · High: Changes to Earth’s Surface — Volcanic Eruptions (Gr 4)

Stretch: Geosphere, Biosphere, Hydrosphere & Atmosphere (Gr 5) · Changes to Earth's Surface: Erosion (Gr 5)

Review / catch-up — revisit last week's Explorer learning; finish unfinished investigations or SeeSaw posts; preview this week's topic.

Mon · Teach + Build

▶ Hook video: Earthquakes 101 + Volcanoes 101 (NatGeo, ≈4 min each)

Teach (15 min, kept): Earth’s 4 systems (geosphere/hydrosphere/atmosphere/biosphere) & which disasters hit each.

Slides: Earthquakes PowerPoint (Padlet · Earthquakes column)

Prior-year printables: All About Volcanoes PowerPoint (Padlet · Volcanos) · KS2 Earthquakes Fact File (Padlet · Earthquakes)

Epic reads (Gr 2-4, new): Earthquakes, Eruptions, and Other Events that Change Earth · Volcano!

Objective: Name Earth's four systems (geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, biosphere) and identify which natural disaster is linked to each.

Assessment: Match a disaster (earthquake, flood, storm, volcano) to the Earth system it comes from.

Differentiation: Match one system to one disaster using labelled pictures with support / match two or more systems to disasters with some support / name all four systems and their linked disasters independently.

Extra (new): BrainPOP: Volcanoes and Twinkl: Plate Tectonics Interactive Map for extra plate-tectonics practice if time allows.

Build (25 min): design & sketch your disaster-proof house; plan materials.

Objective: Plan and sketch a design for a disaster-proof house, choosing materials suited to withstanding a natural disaster.

Assessment: Produce a labelled sketch that shows the house shape and lists the materials chosen, with a reason for at least one material choice.

Differentiation: Sketch and label with a materials word bank and adult support / sketch and choose materials with some support / sketch, label, and justify material choices independently.

Tue · Teach + Build

Teach (15 min, kept): earthquakes & why buildings fail; make a bar graph of disaster data (‘which happens most?’).

Printable: Earthquake Cross-Section Labelling (Padlet · Earthquakes column)

Review station (prior year): Earthquake Board Game — board, cards & teacher notes (Padlet · Earthquakes)

Objective: Explain why buildings can fail during an earthquake and represent disaster-frequency data on a bar graph.

Assessment: Build a bar graph from the disaster data and answer: Which disaster happens most? Why might a building fail in an earthquake?

Differentiation: Complete the bar graph with adult support and choose an answer from a word bank / build the bar graph and give one reason for building failure with some support / build the bar graph and explain building failure independently.

Build (25 min): build the base & frame to resist shaking.

Objective: Construct a base and frame designed to resist shaking, applying an understanding of earthquake forces.

Assessment: Test the frame with a gentle shake and describe whether it stayed standing and why.

Differentiation: Build the frame with step-by-step adult support / build the frame and describe if it held with some support / build the frame and explain independently what design choice helped it resist shaking.

Wed · Teach + Build

Teach (15 min): floods & high winds — how do they damage homes? Watch Tornadoes 101 (NatGeo, new) for real high-wind footage.

Reading (Gr 2-3): 'When the Earth Shakes' (Grade 2-3 Readings Pack) · Readings Pack — ADD PADLET LINK

Objective: Explain how floods and high winds damage homes, using evidence from real disaster footage.

Assessment: Name one way a flood and one way high winds can damage a home.

Differentiation: Name one type of damage with a picture/word bank and support / name one way floods and one way wind damage homes with some support / explain how both floods and high winds damage homes independently.

Safety check (new): name one safety action per disaster — earthquake: Drop, Cover, Hold; flood: move to higher ground; storm/tornado: stay indoors, away from windows.

Prior-year activity: Responses to Earthquakes — Activity (Padlet · Earthquakes)

Build (25 min): raise / waterproof & strengthen the house.

Objective: Name a safety action for each disaster type and modify the house design to raise, waterproof, and strengthen it against flood and wind.

Assessment: State one safety action per disaster (earthquake, flood, storm) and show how the house was raised, waterproofed, or strengthened.

Differentiation: Name one safety action with adult support and a picture cue / name two safety actions and make one house improvement with some support / name safety actions for all three disasters and explain the house improvements independently.

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed.
