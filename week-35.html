3-day plan (Mon–Wed)

Build, Test & Showcase the House (Exit Task) — Project/Exit week: final build (short review + build), then test, then the Wednesday showcase.

IXL this week:

Low: Design Knee Pads (Gr 2, engineering) · High: Evaluate Design Solutions to Prevent Flooding (Gr 4)

Stretch: Evaluate Design Solutions: Flooding (Gr 5) · Best Design vs Hurricane Damage (Gr 5)

Review / catch-up — revisit last week's Explorer learning; finish unfinished investigations or SeeSaw posts; preview this week's topic.

Mon · Teach + Build

Review (15 min): what makes a structure strong — wide base, triangles, bracing.

Design brief: Twinkl: Design an Earthquake-Proof Building

Early finishers (prior year): Tectonic Plates Jigsaw, Gr 3-4 (Padlet · Earthquakes)

Objective: Identify design features that make a structure strong, such as a wide base, triangles, and bracing.

Assessment: Name one structural feature in their own house design and explain why it makes the structure stronger.

Differentiation: Point to a strong feature in a picture example with support / name one strengthening feature in their own design with some support / name and explain two or more strengthening features independently.

Build (25 min): finish construction of your disaster-proof house.

Objective: Complete construction of the disaster-proof house, applying structural strengthening features learned this week.

Assessment: Finish the house and point out where a wide base, triangle, or bracing feature was used.

Differentiation: Finish the build with adult support and point to one feature with a picture cue / finish the build and name one strengthening feature with some support / finish the build and identify all strengthening features used independently.

Tue · Test & Improve

Test & improve (full session): run the shake-table / wind / flood test; record what failed and redesign.

Record sheet: House test & redesign record sheet — ADD PADLET LINK

Objective: Test the house against a simulated disaster, record what failed, and redesign to improve its performance.

Assessment: Record the test result, name what failed, and describe one redesign change made to fix it.

Differentiation: Record the result (survived/failed) with adult support / name what failed with some support / record the result, explain the failure, and justify the redesign change independently.

Wed · Showcase (Exit)

Showcase (Exit Task — Wednesday): present & test your disaster-proof house for parents; explain your design. Reflection + Forces/Energy/Earth Systems assessment.

Assessment & rubric: Unit 7 Energy, Forces & Earth Systems Assessment (Padlet · Assessments) · Disaster-Proof House Rubric (new docx) — ADD PADLET LINK

Exit Task

Exit Task — Build & Test a Disaster-Proof House (public-facing) · done on the LAST WEDNESDAY of the unit

Explorers apply what they’ve learned about forces, energy and Earth’s systems to design, build and test a structure that can survive a simulated disaster (a shake-table ‘earthquake’ or wind/flood test), then showcase and explain their design.

Rubric: Disaster-Proof House Rubric (new docx — upload to Padlet) — ADD PADLET LINK

Assessment: ‘Forces, Energy & Earth Systems’ review assessment (Grade 5 Assessments padlet).

Objective: Apply understanding of forces, energy, and Earth's systems to design, build, test, and present a disaster-proof house.

Differentiation: Present one feature of the house with sentence starters and adult support / present two features with some support / present and justify the full design independently, connecting it to forces, energy, or Earth-systems vocabulary.

Review / catch-up — consolidate Mon–Wed learning; complete recordings/reflections; reteach or extend as needed. Celebrate the completed Exit Task.
